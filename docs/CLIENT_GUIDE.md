# PropBot Pro — Sales Agent & Manager User Guide

Welcome to **PropBot Pro**. This guide is designed for real estate sales agents, property consultants, and sales managers using the CRM, Agent Copilot, and Lead Conversion tools.

---

## 1. Understanding Buyer DNA

Every visitor conversing with your website bot or WhatsApp bot is analyzed in real time. PropBot extracts a structured **Buyer DNA** card so you know exactly who you are calling before picking up the phone:

| Attribute | What It Tells You | Sales Tip |
|---|---|---|
| **Budget Range** | E.g. ₹1.2 Cr – ₹1.5 Cr | Never pitch units above maximum budget without financing pre-check |
| **Configuration** | 2 BHK, 3 BHK, Penthouse | Confirms floor plan preference and family size |
| **Purpose** | End-Use vs. Investment | End-users care about schools & amenities; investors care about rental yield |
| **Purchase Timeline** | Immediate (<30 days), 3 months, 6+ months | Focus immediate closers on ready-to-move or nearing possession |
| **Funding Mode** | Bank Home Loan vs. Self-Funded | Offer in-house home loan pre-approval assistance |
| **Possession Stage** | Ready to Move vs. Under Construction | Aligns with buyer urgency and risk tolerance |

## 2. Live Intent Scoring (0 – 100)

The AI continuously recalculates the lead's Intent Score based on engagement depth, visit requests, and qualification answers:

```
[0 - 39: COLD]       ---> Casual browser, vague budget, asking generic city questions.
[40 - 69: WARM]      ---> Specific project inquired, downloaded brochure, shared phone number.
[70 - 100: HOT] 🔥   ---> Requested site visit, confirmed budget & timeline, matched inventory.
```

> **Rule of Thumb**: Call all **HOT (70+)** leads within **5 minutes** of receiving the notification to maximize conversion rates.

## 3. Working the CRM Lead Pipeline

1. **Kanban & List Views**: Organize leads through standard stages: `New Lead`, `Contacted`, `Site Visit Scheduled`, `Site Visit Completed`, `Negotiation`, `Closed Won`, `Closed Lost`.
2. **Filtering**: Sort leads by **Intent Score**, **Assigned Agent**, **Project**, or **Last Activity**.
3. **One-Click Actions**:
   - **Call Lead**: Direct click-to-call or dialer integration.
   - **WhatsApp Chat**: Launch WhatsApp Web with pre-filled greeting.
   - **Send Shortlist**: Generate a personalized Smart Shortlist Room.

---

## 4. Agent Copilot: Pre-Call Hot Lead Brief

When opening any lead detail view, the **Agent Copilot** card appears at the top:

- **30-Second Summary**: A concise 2-sentence synopsis of why the buyer contacted you.
- **Key Talking Points**: Specific amenities or inventory units that match their exact queries.
- **Objection Handling**: AI predictions on potential hesitations (e.g., *"Visitor expressed concern about distance from metro station — highlight upcoming Phase 2 extension"*).

---

## 5. Smart Shortlist Room

Instead of sending static PDFs over WhatsApp that get lost in chat history:

1. Click **Create Shortlist** on the lead profile.
2. Select 2 to 4 units that match their budget and configuration.
3. Click **Generate Room Link** and send to the buyer.
4. **Live Activity Tracking**: You receive real-time notifications when the buyer opens the room, compares floor plans, or favorites a specific unit.

---

## 6. Managing Appointments & Site Visits

- **Calendar View**: View all upcoming site visits across day, week, and month views.
- **Visit Confirmations**: Automated WhatsApp messages with Google Maps directions are sent to buyers 24 hours and 2 hours prior to the visit.
- **Post-Visit Notes**: Immediately log visit feedback (`Attended`, `No-Show`, `Interested in Unit 402`) to update the buyer's pipeline stage.

---

## 7. Taking Over Live Chat (Human Handoff)

If a buyer requests human assistance or asks a complex negotiation question:
1. You receive a desktop/mobile push notification: *"Buyer requesting human agent"*.
2. Open **Live Chat** in the CRM dashboard.
3. Click **Take Over Chat**.
4. The AI seamlessly pauses. Type directly to the buyer in real time.
5. Once resolved, click **Return to AI** to let the bot resume automated follow-ups.
